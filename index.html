How to play

  1. Find a partner who has the other role. If you both got the same role, one of you taps “Switch role”.
  2. In each scenario, read the story and the possible outcomes, then choose. Do not talk.
  3. Show each other your screens, then press “Reveal”.
  4. Each outcome says how good it is for each side: Best, Good, Bad or Worst. Your own side is highlighted.

Five words you will need

Player: one side in the game, the protesters or the police.
Strategy: a choice a side can make, such as a peaceful march or a crackdown.
Payoff: how an outcome ranks for a side: Best, Good, Bad or Worst.
Best reply: your choice that gives you the better result, given what the other side does.
Equilibrium: a pair of choices where neither side could do better by changing only its own choice.
The scale, for each side: Best›Good›Bad›Worst

The rankings are illustrative: they are made up to show how the game works, not measured.

Part 1. Choose at the same time

The protesters choose a peaceful march or disruptive action. At the same moment, the police choose to facilitate or to crack down. Neither side knows what the other will do.

Part 2. Choose one after the other

Now the protesters move first. The police see what they did and then respond, and the public reacts. Each side can look ahead to what the other will do.
