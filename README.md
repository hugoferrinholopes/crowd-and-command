# Crowd and Command

**Live page:** https://hugoferrinholopes.github.io/crowd-and-command/

A classroom game for two players, made for GOVT 3421, *Youth and Politics* (Department of Government, Georgetown University, Fall 2026), by Hugo Ferrinho Lopes. The page assigns each student a role, police or protesters. Students pair with a student who has the other role, choose without talking, show each other their screens, and select Reveal.

- **Part 1:** both sides choose at the same time. Two scenarios (Climate march, Road blockade) and a bonus round (Cat and mouse), each with a step-by-step solution.
- **Part 2:** the protesters move first and the police respond (Road blockade). The solution works back from the end.
- **Quiz:** four questions.

Outcomes are ranked Best, Good, Bad or Worst for each side. The ranks are illustrative, not measurements. The page is one self-contained file, `index.html`, and loads nothing from other hosts.
