# Crowd and Command

**Live page:** https://hugoferrinholopes.github.io/crowd-and-command/

A classroom game for pairs, made for GOVT 3421, *Youth and Politics* (Department of Government, Georgetown University, Fall 2026), by Hugo Ferrinho Lopes. The page gives each student a role, Police or Protesters. Students pair up with someone from the other side, choose without talking, show each other their screens, and press Reveal.

- **Part 1, choose at the same time:** three scenarios (Climate march, Road blockade, Cat and mouse), each with a full step-by-step solution.
- **Part 2, choose one after the other:** the protesters move first, the police respond, and the public reacts. Solved by looking ahead from the end.
- **Quiz:** five quick questions.

Outcomes are ranked Best, Good, Bad or Worst for each side. The rankings are illustrative, not measured. The page is one self-contained file, `index.html`, and loads nothing from other hosts.
