# Crowd and Command

**Live page:** https://hugoferrinholopes.github.io/crowd-and-command/

A small teaching game for GOVT 3421, *Youth and Politics* (Department of Government, Georgetown University, Fall 2026), by Hugo Ferrinho Lopes. Protesters choose between a peaceful march and disruptive action; the police choose between facilitating and cracking down.

- **Play the matrix:** both sides choose at once. Pick a scenario or set your own payoffs, then find each side's best replies and the equilibrium step by step.
- **Play the sequence:** the protesters move first, the police respond, and the public reacts. Solve the game tree by backward induction.
- **Quiz:** five quick questions.

Payoffs are illustrative ranks, not measurements. The page is one self-contained file, `index.html`, and loads nothing from other hosts.
